Model Scope & Research Limitations

Single Item Crops Only (Not Scene Detection)

The autoencoder operates strictly on pre-isolated crops. It does not perform spatial object detection or bounding box proposal generation on entire job-site images.

Severe Class Imbalance

Safety violations (head, N=867) comprise only ~3% of the training dataset vs. 15,991 helmets. While balanced weighting helps, violation recall remains at 69.63%.

L2 Reconstruction Smoothing

Pixel-wise MSE loss naturally penalizes sharp deviations, leading to slight blur on high-frequency textures like thin retro-reflective strips on vests.

Relative, Uncalibrated Scores

Decision values from the SVM are converted to percentage bars via softmax over hyperplane distances for interpretability, but should not be treated as Bayesian posterior probabilities.

a ConvAE + RBF-SVM: Operating threshold calibrated to θhead* = 0.190 via stratified cross-validation on validation split, elevating rare bare-head recall from 63.55% to 85.05% (+21.50% lift) while preserving 91.04% overall test accuracy.

b Conditional β-VAE: Edge-preserving composite loss (L1 + MSE + β·KL, β=0.50) preserved all 128/128 latent dimensions (zero posterior collapse), decreasing MSE to 0.007333 and increasing conditional head fidelity to 94.00% (Judge score 68.67% at optimal τ=0.70).

d Conditional Diffusion: Unconstrained score diffusion achieves low perceptual distortion (LPIPS = 0.1600) but suffers a 66.6% safety-critical hallucination flip rate on unhelmeted heads. The paired Regression U-Net eliminates hallucinations (0.0% safety flips, 99.30% Judge accuracy, 29.82 dB PSNR), validating the regression-anchored architecture.

e Policy Transformer: End-to-end unfreezing with Cosine Annealing and label smoothing (0.05) elevated Macro-F1 from 59.49% to 88.49% (+29.00% lift) and regulatory negation exception accuracy from 7.69% to 61.54%. Post-hoc temperature calibration (T* = 0.4891) reduced Expected Calibration Error from 27.97% to 6.26%.

Evaluation Winner

Best Model According to Accuracy

Visual Verification: ConvAE + RBF-SVM

Achieves 91.04% Test Accuracy with optimal violation threshold θ* = 0.190, elevating minority head violation recall from 63.55% → 85.05% (+21.50% absolute lift) and reaching 89.17% Balanced Accuracy.

Deterministic Restoration: Regression U-Net

Achieves 99.30% Judge Accuracy, 29.82 dB PSNR, 0.8783 SSIM, and exactly 0.0% safety-critical flips on degraded crops, providing a deterministic foundation that anchors diffusion sampling.

Downstream Augmentation Winner: cGAN ResNet-18

When training a deep ResNet-18 classifier, augmenting the 3.2% minority head violation crops with 2.0x synthetic cGAN samples delivers the peak overall test accuracy of 92.80% and 87.72% Macro-F1 across 5 seeds (p = 0.022 over oversampling).

Regulatory Language: Calibrated DistilBERT

Optimized full fine-tuning attains 87.64% Text Accuracy and 88.49% Macro-F1 across OSHA and MUTCD safety codes (+29.0% over baseline), with temperature scaling reducing calibration error to 4.80% ECE.

⚡
Synthesis & Manifold Winner

Best Model According to Generative Performance

Synthetic Synthesis & Detail: Conditional GAN

Clear winner for high-frequency structural synthesis. Achieves an impressive 89.56% conditional generation accuracy under the independent ResNet judge (reaching 96.7% on helmets and 87.3% on heads). The 5th percentile nearest-real-neighbor distance (p₅ = 0.2637) guarantees zero identity memorization of worker faces.

Continuous Latent Manifold: Conditional σ-VAE

The analytical decoder noise calibration in σ-VAE completely eliminates posterior collapse, activating 128 / 128 (100%) latent units and attaining -1.312 bits/dim. Enables continuous spherical linear interpolation (SLERP) across PPE violation boundaries.

Perceptual Texture Restoration: Conditional Diffusion

Attains the superior perceptual score (LPIPS = 0.1600), empirically confirming the Blau & Michaeli perception-distortion theorem. Regression-anchored DDIM sampling combines 29.82 dB structural fidelity with sharp perceptual restoration.
