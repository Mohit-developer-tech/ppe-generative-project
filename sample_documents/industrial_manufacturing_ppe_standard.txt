# GLOBAL INDUSTRIAL MANUFACTURING FACILITY - PPE SAFETY MANDATE
# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) - PLANT OPERATIONS 2026

SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This standard operating procedure establishes mandatory personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements for all employees, maintenance technicians, contractors, and visitors operating within manufacturing fabrication bays, assembly lines, and material handling warehouses.

SECTION 2: TORSO PROTECTION AND HIGH-VISIBILITY VEST RULES
All personnel operating or walking within designated forklift corridors, automated guided vehicle (AGV) zones, and loading docks must wear an approved ANSI Class 2 high-visibility safety vest at all times.
High-visibility vests must be brightly colored fluorescent yellow-green or orange with silver reflective striping to guarantee contrast against indoor industrial machinery.
Loose or unzipped vests are strictly prohibited near rotating shafts, conveyor drives, or automated pick-and-place equipment due to entanglement hazards.

SECTION 3: HEAD AND FACE PROTECTION STANDARDS
Industrial hard hats or bump caps certified to ANSI Z89.1 must be worn in all areas where overhead hoists, automated gantry cranes, or heavy pallet racking are installed.
Protective safety glasses with side shields meeting ANSI Z87.1 must be donned immediately upon entering any manufacturing or assembly hall.
Face shields must be worn in conjunction with safety glasses during all grinding, plasma cutting, or battery maintenance tasks.

SECTION 4: COMPLIANCE ENFORCEMENT & EXCEPTIONS
Personnel working exclusively inside enclosed climate-controlled control rooms, quality assurance laboratories, or administrative office corridors are exempt from high-visibility vest and head protection requirements.
Failure to comply with mandatory PPE protocols in designated active zones will result in disciplinary safety review and immediate suspension from the facility floor.
All damaged, cracked, or soiled PPE items must be surrendered to the safety department for immediate complimentary replacement.
