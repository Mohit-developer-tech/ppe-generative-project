# FEDERAL MUTCD PART 6D & DOT HIGHWAY SAFETY STANDARD
# MANDATORY PERSONAL PROTECTIVE APPAREL IN TEMPORARY TRAFFIC CONTROL ZONES

SECTION 1: APPLICABILITY & REGULATORY AUTHORITY
This compliance standard applies to all transportation agency personnel, utility contractors, and survey crews performing operations within the right-of-way of Federal-aid highways and municipal arterial roads.
All individuals present within the temporary traffic control (TTC) zone shall be equipped with approved personal protective equipment meeting current Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) guidelines.

SECTION 2: MANDATORY HIGH-VISIBILITY SAFETY APPAREL
For all daytime and nighttime work activity on highway shoulders or travel lanes, all personnel must wear ANSI/ISEA 107-2020 Class 2 or Class 3 high-visibility safety garments.
High-visibility vests must feature fluorescent yellow-green or orange-red background material with retroreflective bands placed horizontally and vertically over the torso and shoulders.
Nighttime flaggers must wear ANSI Class 3 garments consisting of a high-visibility vest or jacket paired with high-visibility retroreflective trousers.
High-visibility vests shall not be obscured by tool belts, backpacks, or outerwear that lacks retroreflective certification.

SECTION 3: HEAD PROTECTION SPECIFICATIONS
All highway workers within the drop zone of overhead bridge structures, tree clearing machinery, or paving equipment must wear an approved ANSI Z89.1 Type I industrial hard hat.
Hard hats must be worn with the brim facing forward and properly adjusted chin straps in high-wind conditions above 25 mph.
Contractors operating enclosed rollers or asphalt pavers are exempt from wearing hard hats while remaining inside the ROPS-shielded enclosure.

SECTION 4: DUAL COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS (VEST & HELMET)
During active asphalt milling, nighttime lane closure setup, and guardrail installation, both a hard hat and a high-visibility vest must be worn simultaneously by all crew members.
The safety supervisor shall verify dual PPE compliance before allowing any worker into the active travel lane closure.

SECTION 5: GENERAL SAFETY CONTEXT & EXCEPTIONS
Routine vehicle refueling inside designated contractor staging yards does not require high-visibility vests if the yard is physically separated from public traffic by concrete Jersey barriers.
All reflective safety garments that have lost retroreflectivity due to laundering, abrasion, or dirt accumulation must be destroyed and replaced immediately.
