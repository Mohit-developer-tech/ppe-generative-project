# OSHA 29 CFR 1926 - CONSTRUCTION SAFETY COMPLIANCE POLICY
# DIVISION OF INDUSTRIAL SAFETY & HEALTH - PPE MANDATE 2026

1. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS (29 CFR 1926.100 - HEAD PROTECTION)
Employees working in areas where there is a possible danger of head injury from impact, or from falling or flying objects, or from electrical shock and burns, shall be protected by protective helmets.
All helmets must comply with ANSI/ISEA Z89.1-2014 Type I or Type II industrial head protection standards.
Hard hats must be worn at all times by all personnel, subcontractors, and visitors entering the active construction zone beyond the site perimeter gate.
Exceptions to hard hat wear are strictly limited to operators inside fully enclosed, ROPS-certified cab machinery with windows closed, or inside designated temporary construction trailers.

2. HIGH-VISIBILITY SAFETY APPAREL (29 CFR 1926.200 & 1926.201 - TORSO PROTECTION)
All workers exposed to public vehicular traffic, earthmoving machinery, or mobile heavy equipment must wear high-visibility safety apparel complying with ANSI/ISEA 107-2020 Performance Class 2 or Class 3.
High-visibility vests, jackets, or shirts must incorporate retroreflective material providing 360-degree visibility under daytime and nighttime lighting conditions.
Flaggers and signal persons stationed near active roadways or crane swing radii must wear Performance Class 3 high-visibility vests at all times.

3. DUAL-PROTECTION PROTOCOL (HEAD AND TORSO MANDATE)
In all active excavation trenches, crane lift paths, and structural steel erection zones, both protective helmets (hard hats) and high-visibility safety vests are mandatory without exception.
No person shall enter an excavation deeper than 5 feet without donning an approved ANSI hard hat and high-visibility apparel.
Failure to wear both hard hat and vest in designated dual-mandate zones constitutes a Class A safety violation subject to immediate site removal.

4. SPECIALIZED AND EYE PROTECTION
Protective eye and face equipment shall be provided where there is a risk of flying particles, chemical hazards, or molten metal splash.
Hearing protection devices must be worn whenever ambient noise levels exceed 85 dBA time-weighted average over an 8-hour shift.
Safety shoes or boots with impact-resistant steel or composite toe caps meeting ASTM F2413 specifications are mandatory across the entire jobsite.

5. NON-COMPLIANCE & EXCEPTION GUIDELINES
Personnel are not required to wear high-visibility garments while seated inside personal passenger vehicles outside the barricaded work boundary.
Head protection is not required within administrative office complexes or enclosed lunch break facilities.
Any damaged, cracked, or chemically compromised PPE must be removed from service and replaced immediately before resuming work.
